What is ReuseReady?

A guided assistant that checks whether a research resource — a dataset, DOI or repository link — is FAIR, responsibly licensed and safe to reuse, and helps you publish your own resources so others can reuse them. Built on the IP4OS Knowledge Valorisation framework.

1
Describe — write your scenario and paste the resource links.
2
Assess — FAIR (via the F-UJI standard) plus AI Readiness and Responsible Licensing, and the declared licence.
3
Plan — the metadata to add, licence guidance and next steps.

Where AI is used: only to word the explanations — never to decide the scores. Everything that determines FAIRness, rights or reuse is a fixed, traceable rule.

⚠
Not legal advice. Results are a draft to discuss with a qualified IP or legal advisor.
